My Day – Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 31, 2026

My Day ("the App") is a task and daily planning application. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the App.

1. Information We Store

The App may store information you enter, such as tasks, descriptions, due dates, reminders, recurring tasks, subtasks, and app preferences.

2. Local Storage

Task information and app preferences are stored locally on your device. The App does not require you to create an account.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. When you export or share information using features on your device, that information may be shared through the service or application you select.

4. In-App Purchases

The App may offer in-app purchases. Payments are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment card details. Please refer to Apple's privacy policy for information about its data practices.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Information stored locally remains on your device until you delete it or remove the App. Uninstalling the App may delete locally stored data.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page.
